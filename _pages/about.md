**Welcome to my website!**

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Economics at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.

My research interests lie primarily in **Development Economics, Labor Economics, and Applied Microeconomics**, with additional work in Energy and Environmental Economics. My research examines how public policies and economic incentives shape household decision-making, labor allocation, and economic well-being. My current projects focus on poverty alleviation, structural transformation, and household responses to policy interventions, using quasi-experimental methods, randomized experiments, and structural models to identify causal mechanisms and evaluate policy impacts.
